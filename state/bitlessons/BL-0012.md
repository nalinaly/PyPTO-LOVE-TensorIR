# BL-0012: A target identity is not a legacy backend singleton

Adding an enum member can silently route a new compiler target through an old
platform override, global backend singleton or persisted runtime schema. The
safe first step is a target-only identity with one centralized legacy guard;
do not invent a handler or SoC just to satisfy old APIs.

Hardware traits also do not prove generated-launch legality when a downstream
compiler hard-codes different resource assumptions. The bridge must consume
the traits or independently reject illegal candidates before such a claim.
