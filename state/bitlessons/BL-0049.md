# BL-0049: A compiler cache hit is a validated immutable file, not an existence check

A digest-shaped filename and self-contained Cubin hashes do not authenticate
storage. Derive the name internally from the complete accepted precompile key,
require a trusted owner-private path, and treat only `ENOENT` as a miss. Every
hit must be bounded before allocation, opened without following symlinks,
revalidated against the exact request/spec/producer and compared to its
digest-derived path. Corruption is an error, not a silent recompile or delete.

Publication is not `write(); rename()`. Stage on the same filesystem, sync and
read back the bytes, finalize immutable permissions, sync again, then use a
no-replace atomic primitive. After `EEXIST`, validate exact canonical winner
bytes and sync the shard before acknowledging `AlreadyPresent`. Created
directories require child metadata sync before parent-entry sync.

Keep compilation outside cache coordination. Duplicate compiles are allowed;
atomic publication arbitrates their results. Cache handles may own ordinary
directory descriptors but should be creator-process-bound and never carry CUDA
state. Eviction, repair, authentication and capacity policy are separate
transactions, not convenient side effects of lookup.
