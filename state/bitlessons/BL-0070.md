# BL-0070: A shared policy byte can be an accepted GPU dependency

## Risk

Lowering a CPU-only memory threshold in a shared preflight source appears
unrelated to GPU correctness, but accepted GPU adapters may pin that complete
file. The edit then invalidates their provenance before any test can run.

## Lesson

Preserve exact-hashed base policy bytes. Express the user-authorized 22 GiB
CPU-heavy threshold as a new CPU-only policy/runner family with its own manifest
and evidence. Do not mutate accepted globals, GPU-smoke admission, performance
exclusivity, or historical 24 GiB records.
