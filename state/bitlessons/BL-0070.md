# BL-0070: Operational thresholds are versioned policy, not historical evidence edits

## Risk

A user may refine an approximate resource threshold after earlier control runs
have already frozen the old value. Rewriting those reports would make the past
claim no longer describe what executed.

## Lesson

Version the live policy and leave accepted evidence immutable. CPU-only
coexistence policy v2 lowers admission and resume from 24 GiB to the
user-authorized 22 GiB while retaining the 16 GiB owned-run pause floor and all
process/GPU isolation rules. GPU-smoke and performance policies remain separate.
