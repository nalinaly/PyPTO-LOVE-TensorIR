# BL-0058: Optional-backend builds cannot prove an identity they do not embed

## Risk

A validation harness can accidentally turn a correct backend-disabled product
into a false failure by requiring private NVIDIA build metadata that is only
compiled into the backend-enabled product.

## Lesson

Exact-product validation must distinguish source identity from compiled
capability identity. For the backend-enabled DSO, require the exact PyPTO and
private toolchain revisions. For the backend-disabled DSO, require the exact
loaded DSO path and `compiled=false`; an empty private-backend revision is the
expected fail-closed state, not evidence that the tested source is stale.

The shared canonical schedule behavior is still tested in both products. Do
not weaken the enabled-product identity check to accommodate the disabled one.
