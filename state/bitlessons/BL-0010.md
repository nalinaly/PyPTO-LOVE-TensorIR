# BL-0010: Semantic ABI is not a fast-path accident

An optimized baseline may temporarily quantize an intermediate even when the
model semantics keep it in FP32. ABI semantics must follow the model/reference,
not one fused kernel's implementation artifact. GDN therefore accepts BF16
update preactivation but computes and retains sigmoid beta in FP32.

Stateful operators also lease a generation, not two unrelated pointers. Conv
and recurrent views must represent the same token boundary, be prepared and
copied together, and remain exclusively owned until stream completion. A host
shape/index validator cannot prove that lifecycle.
