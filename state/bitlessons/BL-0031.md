# BL-0031: Bind resumed bytes to one remote representation

Matching URL, offset and total size does not prove that two HTTP responses are
the same object. A premature EOF could otherwise concatenate two same-sized
versions and produce a reviewable but false provenance claim. Capture a strong
ETag and effective HTTPS URL from the initial response, send `If-Range` with
every resume, and require the identical ETag, URL, range and total before any
append. Final size/hash and archive validation remain mandatory.
